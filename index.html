Targeted Negotiation Scripts

Opening: set the tone
"I like the truck and I'm ready to buy today if the numbers work. I've done a full check, so let's go through what I found."
If the shocks or springs failed the test
"The rear dampers are misting and it keeps bouncing after the push test. That's a set of shocks plus fitting I'll need to pay for straight away. I need that reflected in the price."
If a key fob is missing
"There's only one fob here. A replacement is over £250 to program, so either you supply a second key or we take that off the price."
If service gaps show up
"A couple of these services were stretched past the interval. At 93,000 miles that adds risk on the timing chain and transfer box. That's worth a reduction, or a fresh service before I collect."
Asking for extras instead of cash
"If you can't move on price, I'd want a full service, a fresh MOT, and a 12-month warranty included before I sign."
The walk-away line
"I've shown you what needs doing. That's my best offer. If it doesn't work for you, no problem, here's my number if anything changes."
